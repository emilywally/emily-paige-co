Where contemporary architecture meets effortless luxury.

Welcome to 3150 S Ventura Dr, a beautifully reimagined 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath, approximately 2,700-square-foot ranch in the heart of New Berlin. Thoughtfully redesigned with an emphasis on contemporary architecture, sophisticated materials, and seamless functionality, this residence delivers an elevated living experience where every detail feels intentional.

Step inside to discover an expansive, light-filled interior defined by open sightlines, oversized windows, warm wood flooring, ambient lighting, and a striking balance of natural textures and modern finishes. The reconfigured open-concept layout creates an effortless connection between the living, dining, and kitchen spaces, offering an atmosphere that feels both architecturally impressive and exceptionally inviting.

At the heart of the home, a showstopping designer kitchen features sleek two-toned cabinetry, a dramatic full-slab waterfall island, integrated appliances, and a built-in gas cooktop. Clean architectural lines, contrasting finishes, and carefully curated details create a space equally suited for everyday living and sophisticated entertaining.

The private primary suite offers a serene retreat, complete with a beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom and walk-in closet. Throughout the home, three full bathrooms and an additional powder room showcase elevated design through large-format tile, statement finishes, warm wood accents, contemporary fixtures, and refined detailing.

The thoughtfully finished lower level extends the home’s living possibilities, offering flexible space for entertaining, relaxing, working, or hosting guests. Modern lighting, cohesive finishes, and generous living areas carry the home’s contemporary aesthetic throughout.

Outside, the transformation continues with an inviting patio, fire pit area, and spacious backyard designed for outdoor gatherings, quiet evenings, and effortless enjoyment. Updated windows, thoughtful architectural enhancements, and carefully selected materials complement the home’s modern character.

Ideally situated in an established New Berlin neighborhood with convenient access to I-43, I-94, parks, shopping, and dining, this exceptional ranch combines the comfort of suburban living with the sophistication of design-forward architecture.

Bold in design. Warm in character. Exceptional in every detail. This is contemporary ranch living, beautifully redefined.
