Greenfield, a mid-market southwest suburb.

A realtor's notes on Greenfield. What it costs, what's there, and what's worth knowing before you buy or sell here.

Greenfield is a city in Milwaukee County, positioned in the southwest ring of Milwaukee suburbs, bordered by West Allis to the north, Greendale to the south and east, and the City of Milwaukee to the east. The city covers 11.52 square miles (11.51 of it land), with the Root River running through its western portion. As of the 2020 census, Greenfield had 37,803 residents. Greenfield incorporated in 1957, making it the last municipality in Milwaukee County to do so.

The School District of Greenfield operates Greenfield High School, Greenfield Middle School, and four elementary schools: Edgewood, Elm Dale, Glenwood, and Maple Grove, plus 4K/5K early learning programs. Portions of Greenfield's western edge fall within the Whitnall School District instead.

Freeway access is a defining Greenfield feature. Interstate 894 (the Airport Freeway / Zoo Freeway bypass around downtown Milwaukee) enters the city after leaving West Allis and connects the Zoo Interchange to the Mitchell Interchange, giving residents a short drive to Milwaukee Mitchell International Airport and I-94 to Chicago. Interstates 41 and 43 also serve the city.

Retail and outdoor amenities are a mix of the practical and the local. Southridge Mall (the mall footprint sits in Greendale and Greenfield) is Wisconsin's second-largest at about 1.18 million square feet. The Powerline Trail, opened in 2022, is a multi-use path for walking, running, and cycling that crosses the city. Alan Kulwicki Memorial Park honors the 1992 NASCAR Winston Cup champion and Greenfield native.

What Greenfield costs right now

Movoto reports a June 2026 median sold price for Greenfield of about $350,000 across 82 sales, with a median 13 days on market and about $204 per square foot. Greenfield sits solidly in the middle of the southwest suburbs' price range.

Who tends to like Greenfield

Not who "should" live here. Just what kind of daily life this place fits.

If you want a short commute to downtown Milwaukee or Mitchell Airport without living in the city, Greenfield's I-894 access puts both within a straightforward drive.
If you want a suburb with an established housing stock at a mid-market price point, Greenfield's June 2026 median was $350,000 across a mix of single-family homes, condos, and multi-family units, with typical homes around 1,557 square feet.
If you want big-box and mall shopping a few minutes from your driveway, Southridge Mall, with Macy's, JCPenney, Dick's Sporting Goods, and Round 1, sits on Greenfield's southern edge.
If you want walk and bike infrastructure and park access as part of daily life, the Powerline Trail (opened 2022) crosses the city and Milwaukee County parks like Kulwicki add 28+ acres of green space along the Cold Spring Road corridor.
