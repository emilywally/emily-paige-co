Oak Creek, a suburb building its own downtown.

A realtor's notes on Oak Creek. What it costs, what's there, and what's worth knowing before you buy or sell here.

Oak Creek is a city of roughly 36,500 residents in southern Milwaukee County, occupying the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan immediately south of the City of Milwaukee. It incorporated as a city in 1955 as a defensive move against Milwaukee annexation and has grown from a semi-rural community of about 4,800 people in 1950 into one of the fastest growing municipalities in Milwaukee County, reaching 34,451 residents in 2010 and 36,497 in the 2020 census. That trajectory has translated into a comparatively new housing stock, with steady infill and subdivision development continuing on the west and south sides of town.

The community's civic and social center is Drexel Town Square, an 85-acre mixed-use development built on the site of the former AC Spark Plug factory. Anchored by the Oak Creek City Hall, the public library, and a public amphitheater and lawn, Drexel Town Square combines apartments, restaurants, a hotel, medical offices, retail, and a farmers market into a walkable downtown that did not exist a generation ago. It won the Milwaukee Business Journal's 2016 Vision Award.

Public schools serve most of the city through the Oak Creek-Franklin Joint School District, which operates eight elementary schools, two middle schools, a 9th-grade center, and Oak Creek High School.

Transportation is a defining feature: Interstate 94 runs north-south along the city's western edge, connecting Oak Creek to downtown Milwaukee in roughly 15 minutes and to Chicago in about 75 minutes, while I-794 links the northern edge of the city into Milwaukee's lakefront freeway system. General Mitchell International Airport sits just across the northern border in Milwaukee.

What Oak Creek costs right now

Zillow's average home value for Oak Creek is about $418,000 as of May 2026, up roughly 6 percent year over year. Redfin's most recent median sale price sits at about $386,400 as of November 2025. New construction is a real share of Oak Creek's market, so the mix affects the number you see reported.

Who tends to like Oak Creek

Not who "should" live here. Just what kind of daily life this place fits.

If you want newer construction and a walkable downtown that most Milwaukee suburbs don't offer, Oak Creek's Drexel Town Square district puts city hall, restaurants, brewpubs, and the library within a few blocks of newer apartments and single-family neighborhoods.
If you want quick freeway access to downtown Milwaukee, Mitchell Airport, and the drive to Chicago, Oak Creek's location on I-94 and I-794 makes commuting and travel unusually easy for a lakefront community.
If you want Lake Michigan access without paying North Shore prices, parks like Bender Park and Lake Vista Park offer beach, bluff-top trails, and a public boat launch inside city limits.
If you want big-box shopping and everyday errands close to home, the I-94 corridor between Ryan Road and Drexel Avenue concentrates IKEA, Meijer, Costco, Target, and the Marcus South Shore Cinema within a few minutes of most neighborhoods.
