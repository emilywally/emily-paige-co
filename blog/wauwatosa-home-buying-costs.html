How much does it cost to buy a home in Wauwatosa?

The price is the headline. The cash you need on closing day is the number that decides your search.

Plan on a price near $430,000. Resideline reported $430,000 as the median sold price across 249 Wauwatosa closings in the six months before September 2026, with the middle half of sales between $360,000 and $565,000. On a $430,000 home, your down payment is $15,050 at 3.5% down, $21,500 at 5% down or $86,000 at 20% down, and closing costs, an inspection and repairs come on top of that.

That is a median, so half of Wauwatosa homes sold for less and half for more. The wide middle range is the real story: a smaller home near the Village and a larger home on a street along the Menomonee River Parkway are different budgets, so decide which one you are shopping for before you pick a number.

How much is the down payment on a Wauwatosa home?

It depends on your loan, so here is the math on a $430,000 home. Your lender can tell you which programs you qualify for, and a smaller down payment means a larger loan and a larger monthly payment, so look at what you are comfortable paying each month before you chase the lowest cash number.

  • 3.5% down: $15,050
  • 5% down: $21,500
  • 20% down: $86,000

What other costs come with buying in Wauwatosa?

Ask your lender for a Loan Estimate before you start touring seriously. It puts the lender's charges and the title company's charges on one page, so you can see your cash to close instead of guessing at it.

Budget separately for a home inspection. Wauwatosa has plenty of older homes, so look closely at the electrical, the plumbing, the roof and the basement, and ask the inspector about anything that looks original to the house. Whatever the inspection finds is a conversation with the seller before you are committed, which is the point of doing it.

Then set aside money for the first months in the home. Moving costs, a few repairs and the first property tax and insurance payments tend to land at the same time, and they are easier to handle when you planned for them.

How does Wauwatosa compare to the rest of Metro Milwaukee?

The Greater Milwaukee Association of REALTORS® (GMAR) put the four county average sale price at $497,552 in August 2026, in its report released September 14. That is an average, not a median, so it does not line up exactly with the Resideline median above, and the two should be read as a rough comparison only. Wauwatosa sits close to downtown, about a 12 minute drive, which is part of why buyers compare it with the East Side and Shorewood as well as with West Allis.

Should I get pre-approved before I start looking in Wauwatosa?

Yes, and before your first showing if you can. Homes in a desirable price range can move quickly, and a letter from your lender tells you your real budget and tells the seller you are ready. Our post on Wisconsin mortgage pre-approval walks through what lenders ask for.

What should I do next?

Pick a monthly payment you are comfortable with, work backward to a price with your lender, and then look at Wauwatosa homes in that range.
