How much does it cost to buy a home in Oak Creek?

Plan on about $367,000, then add closing costs, taxes and the extras the price does not show.

Plan on a price near $367,000 for an Oak Creek home, which puts your down payment at about $12,845 with 3.5% down, $18,350 with 5% down or $73,400 with 20% down. On top of that, budget roughly $7,340 to $11,010 for closing costs, plus money for an inspection and the first few months in the house.

That price comes from Resideline, which reported a median sold price of $367,000 across 225 Oak Creek closings in the six months before September 6, 2026. It is a median, so half of those homes sold for less and half for more, and Oak Creek has a wider spread than most suburbs because so much of what sells here is brand new. A 1960s ranch in the established northern part of the city and a new build south of Ryan Road are two different budgets, so decide which one you are shopping for before you settle on a number.

How much is the down payment on an Oak Creek home?

It depends on your loan, and your lender can tell you which programs you qualify for. A smaller down payment means a larger monthly payment, so start from what you are comfortable paying each month.

  • 3.5% down: $12,845
  • 5% down: $18,350
  • 20% down: $73,400

How much are closing costs when you buy in Oak Creek?

On a $367,000 home, plan on about $7,340 to $11,010. That is the 2% to 3% of the price I suggest budgeting in my cash to close guide, and it is a planning range, not a quote. Your Loan Estimate is where the real number for your loan shows up.

That covers lender fees, title work, the bank’s check on the home’s value, recording fees and prepaid insurance and taxes. The Wisconsin transfer fee and the owner’s title policy are customarily paid by the seller.

Put together, cash to close on a $367,000 Oak Creek home comes to roughly $20,185 to $23,855 at 3.5% down, or $25,690 to $29,360 at 5% down, before the inspection and the move. Lender fees are the part you can shop, so compare two Loan Estimates.

What is the monthly payment on a $367,000 home in Oak Creek?

Freddie Mac’s weekly survey had the 30 year fixed rate at 7.28% for the week of November 19, 2026. At that rate, principal and interest on a $367,000 home with 5% down comes to about $2,386 a month. That is simple math, not a loan quote, and it does not include property taxes, homeowner’s insurance or mortgage insurance, which your lender will add on top.

The tax piece is the one buyers underestimate. The City of Oak Creek lists its 2025 net property tax rate at $16.48 per $1,000 of assessed value,. Assessed value is not the same as the price you pay, so ask for the home’s most recent tax bill rather than doing the math off the list price. If a home were assessed right at $367,000, that rate works out to about $6,048 a year, or roughly $504 a month.

What are homes like in Oak Creek?

Newer than most of what you will see in the first ring suburbs. Oak Creek grew quickly over the last few decades, so the housing splits between established mid century blocks in the northern part of the city and subdivisions built much more recently, with new construction still going up. You trade the plaster walls and built in cabinets of a Bay View or West Allis bungalow for open floor plans, attached garages, bigger yards and mechanicals that have not seen fifty winters.

Daily life here runs on the car, with one exception. Drexel Town Square, built on the site of an old factory along Drexel Avenue, put city hall, the library, restaurants, apartments and an amphitheater within a few blocks of each other, and it is the closest thing Oak Creek has to a downtown. On the lake side, Bender Park on Ryan Road has a boat launch, beach access and bluff trails, and Lake Vista Park has paved paths with elevated views of Lake Michigan. Most buyers I bring here have no idea the lakefront exists.

Getting around is easy. I-94 runs along the western edge of the city, Mitchell Airport sits just across the northern border, and downtown Milwaukee is roughly a 20 minute drive without traffic. If you are touring in winter, drive your future commute at 7:30 on a weekday at least once, because Ryan Road has been under reconstruction near Howell Avenue and traffic changes how a home feels day to day.

Should I buy new construction or an older home in Oak Creek?

Both can make sense, and they cost differently in ways the price does not show. With an older home, the money goes into what the inspection finds: look closely at the roof, the furnace, the windows, the sewer line to the street and anything that looks original to the house. Read the seller’s Real Estate Condition Report line by line, then get your own inspection anyway.

With new construction, the money goes into everything the model home had that your contract does not. Window coverings, landscaping, a finished basement, upgraded flooring and appliances are often extras, and they add up quickly. Read the builder’s contract with someone on your side, ask whether the subdivision has an association and what its dues cover, and get your own inspection even on a brand new house. My post on what association dues cover walks through the questions to ask.

How does Oak Creek compare to the rest of Metro Milwaukee?

It comes in below the metro number. The Greater Milwaukee Association of REALTORS® (GMAR) put the four county average sale price at $460,243 in October 2026, in its report released November 13, with homes taking an average of 27 days to sell. An average and a median do not line up exactly, so read this as a rough comparison. The gap, plus the newer housing, is a big part of why buyers comparing Bay View, St. Francis and Cudahy end up taking a look at Oak Creek too.

What should I do next?

Pick a monthly payment you are comfortable with, work backward to a price with your lender, and get your pre-approval done before you tour. Then decide whether you are shopping for an established home or new construction, because that answer changes your budget more than any rate will. Quick answers

What is the median home price in Oak Creek, WI?

Resideline reported a median sold price of $367,000 across 225 Oak Creek closings in the six months before September 6, 2026. New construction pulls some sales well above it, so treat it as a starting point.

How much money do I need to buy a $367,000 home in Oak Creek?

Roughly $20,185 to $23,855 at 3.5% down, or $25,690 to $29,360 at 5% down. That is the down payment plus 2% to 3% for closing costs, before the inspection and the move.

What is the property tax rate in Oak Creek?

The City of Oak Creek lists its 2025 net property tax rate at $16.48 per $1,000 of assessed value, combining the city, county, school district, sewerage district and technical college shares. Assessed value is not the sale price, so ask for the home’s most recent tax bill.

Is new construction in Oak Creek more expensive than the price shows?

Often, yes. Window coverings, landscaping, a finished basement and upgraded finishes are frequently extras in a builder contract. Ask about association dues, and get your own inspection even on a new home.
