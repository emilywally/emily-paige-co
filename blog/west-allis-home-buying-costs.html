What does it cost to buy a home in West Allis?

The price is only the first number. Here is the rest of the list, with real arithmetic.

A West Allis buyer should plan around a price near $285,000, which means about $9,975 down at 3.5%, $14,250 at 5% or $57,000 at 20%, plus closing costs, an inspection and a repair cushion. Resideline reported that figure as the median closing price for West Allis across 278 sales in the six months before September 6, 2026, with the middle half of sales between $239,000 and $329,000.

For contrast, the Greater Milwaukee Association of REALTORS® (GMAR) put the four county average sale price at $497,552 in August 2026, released September 14. That is an average and the West Allis number is a median, so the two are not a direct comparison, but the gap is the reason many buyers start looking here. Here is how the whole bill adds up.

How much is the down payment on a West Allis home?

It depends on the loan, so run the arithmetic at three levels on a $285,000 home. At 3.5% down you bring $9,975. At 5% down it is $14,250. At 20% down it is $57,000. Your lender will tell you which programs you qualify for, and the monthly payment you are comfortable with matters more than the smallest possible down payment.

What other costs come with buying?

Think of the down payment as the biggest line, not the only one. Your lender sends a Loan Estimate that lists its own charges and the title company’s charges, and I would ask for it before you tour seriously so the numbers are real. A home inspection is yours to pay for and is worth doing on every home. The bank’s check on the home’s value is also a buyer cost. You will put down earnest money with your offer, which is credited back to you at closing, and you will pay the first year of homeowner’s insurance and your share of the property taxes up to the closing day.

I do not quote a closing cost percentage here because it moves with the loan and the lender. Ask two lenders for a Loan Estimate on the same price and compare the totals.

What should I budget for repairs in an older West Allis home?

West Allis grew up around a large factory in the early 1900s, and the housing stock reflects that: bungalows, Cape Cods and worker’s cottages on gridded blocks, with ranches from the 1950s and 1960s mixed in. The price point is real, and so is the age. At the inspection I look closely at the electrical (some blocks still have knob and tube wiring), the sewer line from the house to the street, plaster walls that can hide surprises, the roof and the furnace.

Wisconsin sellers generally provide a Real Estate Condition Report under state law (Wis. Stat. 709.02), usually within 10 days of an accepted offer, and it is worth reading line by line. It tells you what the seller knows, not what an inspector will find, so you still want your own inspection. A repair cushion on top of your down payment is the most useful cost most buyers forget.

Which West Allis streets should I look at?

West Allis is a block by block search more than most of the metro, and two streets over can feel like a different place. Greenfield Avenue is the on foot spine, with independent restaurants, breweries and bakeries, and the West Allis Farmers Market runs three days a week in season. State Fair Park sits in the middle of the city and hosts the Wisconsin State Fair every August. I-94 and I-894 both run through West Allis, which makes the trip to downtown Milwaukee or Mitchell Airport a short one. Tour the residential blocks north and south of Greenfield, not just the avenue itself.

How fast do homes sell in West Allis?

GMAR reported that homes across the four county metro went under contract in an average of 19 days in August 2026. That is a metro average, not a West Allis figure, but it is a fair reminder that a well priced home does not wait while you gather paperwork. Getting your pre-approval and your Loan Estimate done before you tour is what lets you move when the right one appears.

Quick answers

What is the median home price in West Allis?

Resideline reported a median closing price of $285,000 for West Allis over the six months before September 6, 2026, across 278 sales, with the middle half of sales between $239,000 and $329,000. That is a six month median from one data provider, not a single month figure, so use it as a range.

How much is a down payment on a West Allis home?

On a $285,000 home, 3.5% down is $9,975, 5% down is $14,250 and 20% down is $57,000. Which percentage you need depends on the loan program, so ask your lender which ones fit your situation.

What should I budget for besides the down payment?

Plan for the lender and title charges listed on your Loan Estimate, a home inspection, the bank’s check on the home’s value, the first year of homeowner’s insurance, and your share of property taxes at closing. Ask your lender for the Loan Estimate early so these are real numbers, not guesses.

Do older West Allis homes need repairs after purchase?

Often some. Many West Allis homes are bungalows and Cape Cods built long ago, so the electrical, the sewer line to the street, the roof and the furnace deserve attention at the inspection. Set aside a repair cushion on top of your down payment.
