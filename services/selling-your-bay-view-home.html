Selling your Bay View home

A neighborhood-specific guide from a Bay View resident and Realtor who lists here regularly.

Bay View is one of the tightest markets in Milwaukee County. Bungalows and craftsman homes here move fast, and buyers who’ve been priced out of Wauwatosa and Shorewood are keeping demand relentless. When it’s time to sell, the difference between a great outcome and a merely good one is preparation and pricing, not luck.

How I approach a Bay View listing

I start with a walk-through, in your home, on your schedule. Not to talk pricing yet, but to see what you have. Then I put together a comparative market analysis that’s specific to your block and your finish level, not the countywide average. Bay View has real price variance depending on lot, updates, and proximity to KK. The differences matter.

What I’ll actually do for you

Prep advice, without turning your house into a project. I’ll tell you what’s worth doing (the ten things that return their cost several times over) and what’s not (the twenty things HGTV would have you do that don’t move the needle). Photography, staging referral if it’ll help, and a marketing plan that gets your home in front of the buyers actually shopping in Bay View, not the whole state.

What Bay View sellers are asking me right now

How long will it take? What should I ask? Do I need to fix the roof first, or price it in? Should I entertain offers as they come or set an offer deadline? Should I stay in the house during showings? All of those get real answers, based on what the market for your home actually looks like, not generalizations.

Why I’m the right agent for a Bay View sale

I live here. My auction-house background means I’ve spent a career pricing things people were emotionally attached to, and doing it accurately. And when your listing goes live, my Instagram audience of 20,000+ Milwaukee-focused followers sees it right away. That reach is difficult to replicate.
