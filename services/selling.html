Selling a home in greater Milwaukee.

Pricing, prep, marketing, and negotiation from a team that treats your sale like the closing of a chapter, not a listing.

Selling a home in Milwaukee right now is different from selling one three years ago. Prices have moved. Buyers are more careful. Inspections come back with real requests. The playbook has changed, and pricing your home on hope is the fastest way to end up with a stale listing.

What separates a good sale from a bad one has almost nothing to do with the market or the commission. It has everything to do with whether your agent picks up the phone the night before closing, tells you the truth when the honest answer is hard, and is still around a year later when you are trying to figure out who to call about the water heater. That is what we can actually promise.

What determines the check you walk away with

When you sell, the number that matters is not the fee. It is your net: what lands in your account when everything is said and done. Four things drive that number, and they are not equal in size.

  1. The price your home actually sells for. This is the biggest lever by far. Our listings sell at 108% of list price on average; the market average is 101%. On a $350,000 listing, that gap is nearly $25,000. No fee discount comes close.
  2. How fast it sells. Every extra month on the market is another mortgage payment, another tax bill, more insurance and utilities on a home you are trying to leave. Our listings average 3 days on market versus 14 for the market.
  3. What you give away in negotiations. Inspection responses, closing credits, and appraisal gaps are where sellers quietly lose thousands. Having an experienced negotiator in every one of those conversations protects your bottom line.
  4. What you pay in fees. Yes, this matters. It is just the smallest of the four.

How we approach a listing

The walk-through. We come to your house, on your schedule, before we talk pricing. We go room by room and tell you honestly what is worth doing and what is not: the small repairs that quietly reassure buyers, the fresh paint that earns back multiples of its cost, and the project that is not worth your weekend.

A personalized net sheet before you list. Most sellers do not see one of these until closing day. We build yours before you list, so you know your range from day one and nothing at the closing table is a surprise.

The comparative market analysis. A real CMA of recent comparable sales, similar homes under contract, and your active competition, adjusted for size, style, condition, age, amenities, lot, and location. Online estimates rely on broad averages and can miss your home's reality by a wide margin.

Prep and staging. Trusted cleaners, contractors, handymen, and stagers coordinated on your behalf. Vendor costs are yours, with no markup from us.

Professional photography and video, paid for by us. The photos are the first showing every buyer attends, and they decide whether the second one happens. A listing shot on a phone costs you money on every screen it appears on.

A coming soon campaign, then launch. Anticipation before the listing hits the MLS. Your home is matched against our active buyer database first, then goes to the MLS, thousands of syndicated sites, and our Milwaukee-focused social audience of 60,000+, where our videos have earned more than 20 million views. About 70% of that audience is in the Milwaukee metro.

Every showing scheduled and managed by us, with feedback pursued after each one and passed straight to you.

Offer review and negotiation. Every offer presented clearly, every buyer vetted, every term negotiated. The best offer is not always the highest number. We weigh price, financing strength, loan type, earnest money, inspection terms, and how likely each offer is to actually reach the closing table.

Inspection through closing. Our transaction coordinator tracks every deadline and document with the title company while you pack. Final walkthrough, signatures, funds delivered, keys handed over.

The road, in four phases

Phase one, prepare it. Listing consultation, personalized net sheet, walk-through, prep and staging plan.

Phase two, price and market it. Pricing strategy from the CMA, professional imagery, coming soon campaign, launch to the MLS and to our audience.

Phase three, show it. Showings and open houses managed for you, offers presented and negotiated, contract accepted.

Phase four, sell it. Inspection and appraisal, contingencies cleared, final walkthrough, closing day.

Questions every seller asks

When is the best time to sell? In Wisconsin, the strongest window runs from March through July. Activity slows after the Fourth of July and drops noticeably by October, then demand rebuilds through winter and hits full momentum by March. If your timeline is flexible, we aim for that window. If it is not, we build the strategy around the calendar you have.

Should I make repairs before selling? Small fixes, yes: leaks, peeling paint, and broken fixtures make buyers wonder what else was not maintained. Bigger renovations need a harder look, because some return their cost and many do not. That is a numbers conversation we have at the walk-through, so you spend where it pays and skip where it does not.

What do I need to disclose? Known issues, up front. Buyers have the right to inspect, and bad news lands far worse after you are under contract. Known problems with appliances, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roof, foundation, property lines, or the deed belong on the Wisconsin Real Estate Condition Report.

What happens if my home does not appraise at the contract price? We go back to negotiations and work to save the deal by adjusting price and terms until both sides can live with it, and usually we do. You always have the right to hold firm, though a second appraisal often lands in the same place. This risk is exactly why we price accurately from the start.

What if I need to buy and sell at the same time? This is the most common worry we hear, and it is exactly where a team that works both sides every week earns its keep. There are more bridges than most sellers realize: coordinating your sale and purchase to close together, negotiating a post-closing occupancy so you can stay briefly after closing, or timing your launch around the home you are pursuing. We map the sequence that fits your finances and your nerves before your home ever hits the market.

How long will it take to sell? It depends on the market at the time of listing and whether the price is realistic, but our listings have averaged days, not weeks. If you need speed above all, we reflect that in the list price and the strategy.
